执行背景 Executive Background

2026年，Meta 针对金融行业引入了更严苛的 AI 审计算法。系统不仅扫描关键词，还会交叉比对视觉暗示及落地页一致性。本标准旨在确保 ZERO Markets 社媒账号在 boost 执行过程中，平衡"账户安全"与"增长效能"。

In 2026, Meta introduced more stringent AI auditing algorithms specifically for the financial sector. The system now scans not only keywords but also performs cross-referencing between visual cues and landing page consistency. These standards are designed to ensure that ZERO Markets' social media accounts balance "Account Safety" with "Growth Efficiency" during the execution of post boosts.

II 次轮 Phase 2

内容匹配评估 Content Matching Assessment

⚑ 原则 ⚑ Principle

从用户视角评估"关注动机"。好的 Boost 帖子应是高质量的"内容钩子"，而非硬广。

Evaluate "Motivation to Follow" from the user's perspective. High-quality Boosted posts should act as "Content Hooks" rather than hard advertisements.

1

形式评估：非教学化 Format Evaluation

筛选标准

Screening Standard

避开纯粹信息堆砌，选择具备叙事性的内容

Avoid pure information dumping; select content with a Narrative structure

好的 Boost 帖子应该是一颗"钩子"。内容过于百科全书式，用户往往直接划走。

A good Boosted post should be a "Hook." If content is too encyclopedic, users tend to swipe away without following.

2

心理博弈：痛点触达 Psychological Maneuvering

筛选标准

Screening Standard

封面标题是否直击用户的"心理账户"？

Does the headline directly hit the user's "Psychological Account"?

用户关注的理由不应是"学到了知识"，而是"找到了共鸣"。

The reason a user follows should not be "learned a fact," but "found resonance."

3

语态判定：对话 vs 教导 Tone: Dialogue vs. Instruction

关键数据

Key Data

"分享视角"完播率和关注转化率通常比"教学视角"高出

"Sharing Perspective" typically sees higher completion and follow-conversion rates vs "Instructional Perspective"

Prioritize content that feels like a cafe chat rather than a lecture from a podium.

筛选标准

Screening Standard
